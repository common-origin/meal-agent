Pantry Pasta

Serves 4 · Ready in 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water.
  2. Warm the oil and garlic, then toss through the pasta & serve.
